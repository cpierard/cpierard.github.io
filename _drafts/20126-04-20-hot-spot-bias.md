# Hot Spot Bias
I've been working in plastic ocean modelling for five years. And I recently finished my contract working for the Ocean Cleanup. I was hired to model coastal disperssion of plastic emmitted from rivers. During this time, I noticed that within the organization there was an assumption that plastic in the ocean gathers in hot spot that can allow for technology to clean it. I never heard of anyone challenging this assumption. They straight up accepted this as a cannon and moved on to develop their capture systems and hot spot models. Maybe I joined the organization late and someone already answered that and I missed? 

Being sorrounded in this hot spot culture, with the expression being repeated over and over again, led me to actually ask myself: "how hot can the hotspot be?" I've been working for five years in this field but for a reason I've never actually asked myself that, or I havent' seen it somewhere.
